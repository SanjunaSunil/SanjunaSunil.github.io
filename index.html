Featured

Google Ads AI Response Evaluation and Guardrails

A complete AI knowledge strategy project built on real Google Ads policy content. Covers all four layers of production AI readiness:

  • Knowledge Architecture: Structured Google Ads policy content into 6 retrieval-ready knowledge chunks with 17 metadata fields, enabling intent-aware retrieval using policy identifiers, trigger conditions, and freshness metadata.
  • Intent Classification: Designed an 8-intent taxonomy with confidence thresholds, response-routing rules, tone guidance, and fallback handling for unclassified queries.
  • Prompt Engineering & Grounding: Developed a versioned system prompt with behavioral constraints and escalation triggers; addressed a simulated hallucination scenario by introducing a 90-day freshness gate for policy citations.
  • AI Evaluation: Built and deployed an LLM-as-a-Judge evaluation tool using Gemini to score AI responses across five dimensions: grounding, relevance, format compliance, escalation accuracy, and tone, with four deployment-readiness verdicts.
