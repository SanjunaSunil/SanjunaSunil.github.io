About

I'm a Senior Technical Writer at SAP Labs India. My core work is what it's always been: plan documentation, understand what users are trying to do, write content that helps them do it, and maintain that content across release cycles. I author in DITA using IxiaSoft CCMS, work with product management, engineering, and UX to get the content right, and coordinate documentation readiness across six releases a year — two major SuccessFactors releases and four quarterly SAP Analytics Cloud releases.

A big part of the writing work comes from customer problems. I analysed 500 support cases to find where the help content was failing, then redesigned those topics as task-oriented procedures and decision-tree flowcharts. That work is associated with a 10% reduction in support cases in the three months after the October 2025 release.

Over the past two years I've also applied AI to the writing work directly. I built a conversation evaluation tool that assesses Joule AI agent responses against 24 quality guidelines, cutting review time from over two hours to about one hour. I created an alt-text prompt now used globally across SAP User Assistance teams, and developed an AI-assisted release-note workflow. I also lead Claude Code training sessions for writing colleagues and mentor them on integrating AI into content workflows.

Before SAP, I wrote developer documentation at Blue Yonder — REST API guides, tutorials, and training content. I tested API requests in Postman before writing them up. At Cognizant, I supported enterprise presales RFPs.
