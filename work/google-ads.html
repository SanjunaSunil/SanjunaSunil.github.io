The Problem

AI responses are only as good as the knowledge behind them

Most AI chatbot projects focus heavily on the model and the prompt — but the knowledge layer is where responses actually succeed or fail. Unstructured knowledge produces vague, ungrounded responses. Without an intent taxonomy, routing rules can't work. Without evaluation criteria, you can't know when the system is ready to deploy.

This project was built as a full-stack demonstration of what production-ready AI knowledge strategy looks like — using real Google Ads policy content as the domain. The goal was to show all four layers working together: structured knowledge, intent classification, system prompt engineering, and rigorous LLM evaluation.

The Process

Four layers, built in sequence

  • Knowledge architecture: Structured Google Ads policy content into 6 retrieval-ready knowledge chunks. Each chunk carries 17 metadata fields including policy identifier, trigger conditions, intent routing, applicable audience, tone guidance, and a freshness date with a 90-day expiry gate for policy citations.
  • Intent taxonomy: Designed an 8-intent classification system covering account suspension, billing queries, policy clarification, campaign setup, appeal guidance, escalation requests, general FAQs, and out-of-scope queries. Each intent has a confidence threshold, response-routing rule, and fallback handling for low-confidence classifications.
  • System prompt engineering: Built a versioned system prompt with behavioral constraints (no speculation on policy outcomes, no legal advice), escalation triggers (account flagged for legal review), freshness gates (refuse to cite policies older than 90 days), and tone rules per intent category.
  • Simulated hallucination & fix: Introduced a deliberate hallucination scenario — a response citing an outdated policy — and documented the fix: adding the 90-day freshness gate to the system prompt and the metadata freshness field to the knowledge chunks.
  • LLM-as-a-Judge evaluation: Built and deployed an evaluation tool using Gemini to score AI responses across five dimensions: grounding, relevance, format compliance, escalation accuracy, and tone. Outputs a four-verdict deployment readiness rating: Deploy-ready, Needs minor fixes, Needs major revision, Do not deploy.
